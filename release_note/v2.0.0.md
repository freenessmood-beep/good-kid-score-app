# Release Notes — v2.0.0

## What's New 🎉

### 🐰 Carrot Transfers
Bunnies can now send carrots to each other! Head to the **Trades** page and switch to the **Transfers** tab to send some carrots to a friend.
- Bunny owners (children with a linked account) can initiate transfers themselves
- Parents can also manage transfers on their behalf

### 🎀 Decoration Shop
A brand-new shop where bunnies can spend their hard-earned carrots on cute decorations like dresses, hair pins, and necklaces!
- Parents can add and manage the decoration catalog
- Bunnies browse the shop and buy decorations with their available carrots
- Owned decorations are shown on each bunny's profile page

### 🏆 Bunny Carrot Ranking
Check out the new **Ranking** page to see all bunnies sorted by their total carrots earned. Top 3 bunnies get a gold, silver, or bronze medal!

### 🗑️ Delete Bunny (Admin)
Parents can now delete a bunny profile directly from the bunny's detail page. A confirmation step is required to prevent accidents.

---

## How to Get Started
1. **Link a viewer account to a bunny** — when adding a bunny, use the "Link to Viewer Account" dropdown to connect a child's login to their bunny. This lets them log in and manage transfers or shop purchases themselves.
2. **Set up the Decoration Shop** — go to the Shop page and add some decorations to the catalog so bunnies have something to spend their carrots on!
3. **Check the Ranking** — visit the Ranking page anytime to see who's leading the carrot race 🥕
